# The machine card

- **The machine card** is `MachineCard` (`components/machine_card.rs`, [decision 175](../../decisions/175-a-card-per-machine-on-the-hubs-dashboard.md)): one machine at a glance on a hub's dashboard. What it tells, and why a machine needs attention, are `dashboard.rs`'s, decided on the host; the component draws them.

  It is an `<article>` named by its heading (`aria-labelledby`), the machine's hostname in a `<bdi>`, at the caller's `level` (2 on the dashboard). On the hub's own card, "This hub" stands under the hostname, in words in Slate Note, so the card's name reads "demo-hub This hub". The heading takes focus from a script only (`tabindex="-1"`), as when the progress bar goes. Then, in this order: the attention mark; the connection's line ("Online now."); the status in the shared `StatusBadge`; the shared progress bar while the machine runs an operation; its facts in a description list (`<dl>`), a time in a `<time datetime>`; then the caller's controls.

  The attention mark, while the caller gives a reason, is the first paragraph after the heading: a Warning icon a screen reader doesn't hear, "Needs attention:" in bold, then the reason's sentence. It is never color alone and never a colored edge or fill (1.4.1); a failure is stated once, by the status badge and these words.

  The progress bar is the shared `Progress`, named by the caller ("Progress of the operation running on demo-desktop"), never announced (decision 83); when it goes, it hands focus to the card's heading. The card itself is never a stop for Tab, nor a control: no part of it but its controls takes a press. Its controls and a running machine's progress bar are its stops, in the order they stand.

  It is flat: the page's color through it, inside a 1px edge in Field Edge with the small corner (`--radius-small`), 1rem of padding and no shadow (Flat Ledger). The caller sets the cards in a list with `CARDS`: a grid of columns at least 18rem wide, or the page's whole width where it is narrower, which wraps by its own width, one column at 320 px with 200 % text (1.4.10) and several side by side on a wide page. Each card fills its row's height, its controls at its foot. Its styling is Tailwind utilities (decision 117), logical ones only.

  The caller's duties under WCAG 2.2 AA, since the card does none of them: a `heading_id` unique on the page; the heading's level in the page's outline (1.3.1); controls named by the machine they act on, as "Open demo-laptop" is (2.4.6); and a progress label that names the machine. It takes no tone and no size: its status is its badge's, and it is drawn at the page's text size.

  The gallery's "Machine card" demo is three cards in the list the dashboard lays them out in: the hub's own, marked "This hub", with its Back up menu; one whose last run failed, which needs attention; and one that runs an operation, its progress bar at 25 percent. `features/ui/components/machine_card.feature` checks the article named by its heading, Tab past the cards to their controls only, the attention mark's words and hidden icon, the progress bar never announced, the flat frame in light and dark colors with axe-core, one column at 320 × 640 with 200 % text and the cards side by side at 1280 × 800; the gallery's axe-core and ARIA snapshot rows pin the rest.
