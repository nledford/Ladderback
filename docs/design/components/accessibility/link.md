# The link

- **The link** is `Link` (`components/link.rs`), for a way to a page of the app or to another site: a native `<a href>`, always underlined, so a link in a line of text is never told by its color alone (1.4.1). Where it goes is parsed once into a `Destination` (`components/link/rules.rs`, decided on the host), never a raw string the component would guess about: a `Route`, a path of this site, starting with one `/` and holding no space or control character (`//host` and `/\host` are other sites), or an `Outside` address, `http` or `https` in any case, with a host. `javascript:`, `data:` and anything else are refused where the page builds the link.
  - *A route* is a plain `<a href>` inside the router, whose location it reads, not the router's `A`. The router takes every same-origin link's press as a route change (the page titled, focus to its `<h1>`, never a reload) and leaves a press with a modifier key, or with another button than the first, to the browser (a new tab or window). `A` sets its own `aria-current` from the path alone, so a link to a page of a list (`/operations?page=2`) would be current on every page of it, and a second `aria-current` would race it. A route is written as the browser keeps it, so the location can match it and it can't leave the site: no empty, `.` or `..` segment and no backslash, plain or percent-encoded; printable ASCII, and a caller percent-encodes what the browser would.
  - *The current page* comes from the router's location, path and query, never a caller's flag that can drift. `Matching::Exact` is current where the path and the query match (an empty query is none, a trailing slash is ignored), for the brand and a page of a list. `Matching::Section` is current on the path or a page below it at a `/`, whatever the query, for the navigation: `/snapshots` on `/snapshots/demo/3f2a`, never on `/snapshots-old`. The router's `A` reads the path alone: it is Section, and with `exact` it is Exact without the query, so the brand's `A exact` at `/` differs from Exact only on `/?query`. A current link has `aria-current="page"` and is bold (The Weight Not Color Rule).
  - *An outside link* has `rel="external noreferrer"` and no `target`. `external` keeps the router off it: an outside address is never routed, even one that turns out to be the app's own, so it is always a page load. `noreferrer`, which implies `noopener`, tells the other site nothing of the page. It opens where the reader is, and the reader's browser offers a new tab. It says it leaves in words and with an icon: "(goes to another site)" (`ui-link-outside`) after its words, out of sight, so its name begins with the words a sighted reader sees (2.5.3), and Lucide's external-link glyph (`Icon`, decorative, `Inherit`, at the text's size) after its words.
  - *The look.* Its tone's color (`--tone`), underlined at a token's thickness (`--link-underline`, never under a pixel), drawn `--link-underline-offset` under the baseline to clear the descenders, with ink skipping, and thicker under the pointer (`--link-underline-hover`); focus keeps the global ring. Under forced colors it is `LinkText`, its underline and icon with it.
  - *Vocabulary.* Tone: `Primary` (Ladder Blue, the default) and `Inherit` (the text around it, still underlined, for a link inside a status sentence or an Alert). No Secondary and no status tone: a link is not a status, and its color would be the only thing that told it apart. No size: a link is the text around it.

  Its color pairs are the contrast gate's link pairs, `--accent` on `--bg` and on `--surface`: 6.70:1 and 6.09:1 in light, 8.76:1 and 7.59:1 in dark. Being underlined, the link needs no 3:1 against the text around it, which only a link told by its color alone does.

  The caller's duties under WCAG 2.2 AA: words that say where it goes, in their sentence or alone (2.4.4); an action is a `Button`, not a link.

  Not taken from daisyUI's link and Flowbite's typography links, the references: an underline only under the pointer, a color per status (Success, Warning and Error links), visited colors (an app's links keep no reading history), links drawn as buttons (a Button that goes somewhere is Button's, or a later decision) and an option to open a new tab.

  The skip link and the error summary's links to its fields stop the press and move focus themselves, which `Link` can't do yet, and the skip link stands outside the router; they keep their own `<a>`. Every other link of the pages is `Link` (#886): the brand (`Exact`, `Inherit`, so it still looks like the brand), the navigation's pages (`Section`), the start page's links, the not-found page's way home, a snapshot's ID in its row, the snapshot page's way back (`Exact`: the navigation marks the section) and the available update's notes (outside).

  A page builds a destination with `Destination::route` or `Destination::outside`, which give `None` for what the rules refuse. A page's own fixed paths always parse, and the scenarios' link counts would catch one that didn't. A snapshot's row builds its address from the daemon's alias and ID (`snapshot_link`) and shows the ID as text where the rules refuse it. An address the daemon sends, as the update's notes, is parsed where it enters the page (`session.rs`), and an update whose notes are no web address isn't shown.

  The gallery's "Link" demo, before the Text field demo, shows a route link, a link to the gallery itself (the current page), an outside link, a link inside a sentence, an `Inherit` link in a status sentence and an outside link right to left. `features/ui/components/link.feature` checks axe and the ARIA snapshot, Enter's route change, the current page, the outside link's name, icon and `rel`, the underline in light, dark and forced colors, the thicker underline under a resting pointer (`ui-tests/support/pointer.ts`, which only moves it) in light and forced colors, and the wrap at 320 px with 200 % text.
