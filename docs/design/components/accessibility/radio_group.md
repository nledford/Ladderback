# The radio group

- **The radio group** is `RadioGroup` (`components/radio_group.rs`), a tenth component, for one choice of a few, such as Settings' **Stay signed in for** (§10.1): a `<fieldset class="choices" role="radiogroup">` named by its `<legend>`, with an optional description under the legend and its error while it has one, under the description, both wired to the fieldset through `aria-describedby`, so a screen reader reads them on entering the group, then one native `<input type="radio">` per choice, all sharing the group's `name`, each with its visible `<label>` beside it as its click target, its box drawn as a checkbox's but round, with a dot in the page's color once chosen.

  Its error marks the group invalid (`aria-invalid` on the `radiogroup`, which ARIA 1.2 supports there and not on a `radio`), and each choice is edged 2 px in the Error tone by a rule keyed on the group's invalid state, not on an attribute of its own.

  It takes `name`, `legend`, `description`, `error`, the choices as value and label pairs (a choice's `id` is `{name}-{value}`), `label_languages`, which pairs the value of a choice whose label is written in another language with that language (`LabelLanguage`: the `lang` and `dir` its `<label>` declares, WCAG 3.1.2), such as a language named in itself, and a `chosen` signal holding the value chosen, none while it holds no choice's value. The browser makes the group one Tab stop, on the choice made or else the first, and the arrow keys move and choose within it, so it handles no keys and announces nothing. Arrowing sets `chosen` at each step, so a page whose choice has consequences saves it with a button of its own, never on change.

  The gallery's "Radio group" section has three demos: three choices and a description, the middle one chosen; **Demo language**, whose choices English, 日本語 and العربية are each marked with their language and direction; and **Demo backup window**, no choice made, with an error. `features/ui/components/radio_group.feature` drives the first and the third by keyboard alone and checks the marks of the second, and `features/ui/components/choices.feature` checks how the radios and checkboxes are drawn.
