# The button

- **The button** is `Button` (`components/button.rs`), a fourteenth component, for an action the page offers: a native `<button>` drawn from the tokens rather than in the browser's own look (decision 113), flat, with one small corner (`--radius-small`). How it is drawn and what a screen reader meets is `components/button/rules.rs`, decided on the host. Its `Content` is words (`Text`), an icon before words (`IconAndText`) or an icon alone (`IconOnly { label }`, the label its `aria-label`), so no button can be built without a name. `Kind::Button`, the default, sets `type="button"` and `Kind::Submit` sets `type="submit"`; the attribute is never left out, since a `<button>` without one sends its form. Unavailable (`disabled`) and busy both set `aria-disabled="true"` and make it ignore presses, its handler uncalled and its form unsent (`takes_press`); never the `disabled` attribute, which would drop focus to the page (A1). A toggle passes `pressed`, exposed as `aria-pressed`.

  It takes three appearances, Filled, Outlined (the default) and Ghost, in every tone of the shared vocabulary, `Secondary` by default. A Filled button has no `Inherit`: its label would need a color checked against every color the text around it could take, so it is drawn Outlined. A Filled label is `--on-fill` on its tone; an Outlined edge and every label are in the tone; a hover is a step darker, lighter in the dark scheme (`--{accent,muted,success,warning,error}-hover`). `scripts/check-contrast.ts` checks each pair in both schemes, on the page and on `--surface`, hovered or not: the weakest are an edge at 4.56:1 (success and warning on the surface, light; 3:1 needed) and a Filled label at 5.02:1.

  State is never color alone: unavailable is 60 % opacity and a not-allowed cursor, pressed bolder with an inset edge (a Ghost button gains its edge). Under forced colors every appearance takes `ButtonFace` with its label and edge in `ButtonText`, so a Ghost button still reads as a button, an unavailable one `GrayText`, and pressed keeps its weight and edge, a pressed Ghost one's edge in `ButtonText` too, not its tone. A busy button is `aria-disabled` too, but keeps `ButtonText` (`rules::button_class` leaves out the `GrayText` utilities while it is busy): it is at work, not unavailable, and its spinner's arc, in the text's color, stands apart from its `GrayText` track.

  Its `size` is its height in `em`, not the shared scale's length, which sizes a glyph (decision 108): `Small` 1.75em, `Medium` 2em (the default), `Large` 2.5em, `XLarge` 3em, its inline padding growing with it, so the smallest is 28 px tall at 100 % text, past 2.5.8's 24 px. The heights and paddings are one set of utilities per size, `Size::control` in `variants.rs` (`--control-height`, `--control-padding`), that a text field takes too. Its icon and spinner stay `Small`, as tall as the text. An icon-only button is square at each size. A label too long for its line wraps inside the button, which grows taller, and in a row of actions it keeps its own height (the row's `[&>.button]:self-center`, `components::ACTIONS`) rather than the row's tallest.

  Its look is Tailwind utilities (decision 117), logical ones only (#931): `rules::button_class` returns the button's own class, its appearance's, its tone's and its size's, its size's control set, and the utilities that draw with them: every button's look, its appearance's colors (`bg-(--tone)`, `text-on-fill`, `border-(--tone)` and their hover, under the pointer and outside forced colors only), its tone's hover color (`--tone-hover`), square or padded, and the forced-colors `GrayText` unless busy. `style.css` keeps one rule: pressed's weight, which the base rule's `font: inherit` on every `<button>` would outrank as a utility, since a rule outside Tailwind's layers outranks any utility. The `button`, `button-label`, `button-spinner` and appearance classes stay as names.

  A busy button shows a spinner (`Inherit`, `Small`, its label hidden), which a screen reader skips with the button's name left as it was. The spinner takes the icon's place, so a button with an icon keeps its width; a button of words alone gets it before them and grows by the spinner and its gap, since its words still say what runs. **A button that must not move gets an icon.**

  The caller's duties under WCAG 2.2 AA, which the component can't meet for it:

  - **One Filled Primary per action row,** its main action (DESIGN.md, Buttons).
  - **Announcing a busy button's wait, and its end (4.1.3),** through the announcer: the button is no live region, as the spinner isn't.
  - **A label that says what the button does,** an icon-only one's above all, since it is its only name; the type can't stop an empty one (4.1.2).
  - **Never a tone alone (1.4.1, A2).** An Error button still says "Delete"; the tone only tints it.
  - **A toggle keeps one name,** its state in `aria-pressed`, as the icon's toggle does.

  Not taken from Flowbite, the reference: gradients, colored shadows, pills, social and brand buttons, its "Dark" color, and links drawn as buttons, which the Link component doesn't take either (below). A button group is #783's, around this one. Every page's and shared component's button is a `Button` (#780), none the browser's own, which closes #775 (axe-core reading Linux WebKit's `ButtonFace` behind a native button). Where a page needs the element itself it passes `id`, `node_ref`, `described_by` (a pause banner, a held review's reason) or `fuller_name`, a row's action named with its row ("Cancel demo-home"), which begins with the words it shows (2.5.3).

  The gallery's "Button" demo shows every appearance in every tone, named in words; every size; an icon before words and an icon-only button at each size; a toggle that makes four buttons busy: three after it, one of words alone, and the submit button of a small form; an unavailable one; and a toggle. The toggle comes before the buttons it makes busy, so the one that grows moves nothing the reader is about to press. `features/ui/components/button.feature` checks axe (at rest, busy and pressed) and the ARIA snapshot, Enter and Space, each appearance's fill, edge and label, the heights at 200 % text, the icon-only name and square, the icon's place in both directions, unavailable and busy staying focusable and ignoring presses, the busy widths, the toggle's state and look, the form sent but never while its button is busy, and forced colors.
