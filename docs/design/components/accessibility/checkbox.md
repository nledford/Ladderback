# The checkbox

- **The checkbox** is `Checkbox` (`components/checkbox.rs`), an eighth component: a native `<input type="checkbox">` with its visible `<label>` beside it, which is also its click target, then under the label an optional description and its error while it has one, the text field's parts with the text field's ids (`{id}-description`, `{id}-error`) and wiring (`aria-describedby`, `aria-invalid`). It takes its `id`, `label`, `description`, `error` (a signal, as a text field's) and a `checked` signal the page reads and resets; Space turns it on and off, and the browser tells a screen reader its state, so nothing announces it. Its error is the page's to say, as a text field's is.

  Its box is drawn from the tokens, as a text field is (decision 118): the browser's own drawing dropped (`appearance: none`), never under 24 px a side, the smallest target 2.5.8 allows, and 1.5 times its text, so it grows with the reader's text; a 1 px Field Edge round the page's color, with a text field's small corner; on, the edge and the fill in Ladder Blue under a Lucide check in the page's color, which a screen reader skips; invalid, the edge 2 px in the Error tone. Under forced colors it takes the reader's `Field` and `FieldText` whether on or off, the check alone telling them apart, and invalid keeps its thicker edge.

  How the box and its mark are drawn is `components/checkbox/rules.rs`, which the radio group shares. It takes no tone (its meaning is in its words, 1.4.1) and no size but its text's. A disabled or indeterminate state waits for a page that needs one. The gallery shows it off with a description, on without one, and off with a description and an error.
