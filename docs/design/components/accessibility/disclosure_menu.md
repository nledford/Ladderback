# The disclosure menu

- **The disclosure menu** is `DisclosureMenu` (`components/disclosure_menu.rs`, decision 152): a few rows behind one button, in a navigation, the header's Settings menu, or on the hub's own machine card, its Back up menu (decision 175). It follows WAI-ARIA's disclosure navigation pattern, not `role="menu"`, which is for an application's commands and makes a screen reader read links as menu items: its rows are the caller's links, and buttons where a row does something (Sign out, a plan's Start backup), which Tab reaches in order after the button, with no arrow keys of their own.

  The button is a native `<button>`, not the shared Button, since it is drawn as the navigation's words beside it: the text's color, the links' size, no box, underlined under the pointer as a link standing alone is. It is named by its words, with a chevron after them as decoration (down while the panel is hidden, up while it shows), `aria-expanded` its state and `aria-controls` its panel's id. While `current` holds, the page the reader is on is one of the panel's, so the button carries `aria-current="true"` and is drawn as a current link is, bold with a 3px bar in the text's color at its inline start: where the reader is shows with the panel closed.

  When it opens and closes is `components/disclosure_menu/rules.rs`, decided on the host; the component only reads the DOM and asks. Enter, Space or a press toggles the panel; the pointer resting on the button never shows it. Escape inside the open menu closes it and returns focus to the button, but only while the button is drawn (`is_drawn`, from its box): a page may hide the button and show the rows in a menu of its own, as the header's hamburger panel does below 54rem, and that menu's Escape is then its own.

  Focus leaving for an element outside the menu closes it without moving focus, so the panel never covers what focus moved to (2.4.11), and focus is never kept in it. Focus going nowhere (a press on something that takes no focus, or in WebKit, which doesn't focus a pressed link or button) leaves it open, so a press inside reaches its row; a press of the pointer outside the open menu closes it on its own.

  A row chosen closes the menu (`focus_returns_on_choice`, 2.4.3). A link's route change then moves focus as every link's does, but the current page's link changes no route and is no longer drawn, so focus goes back to the menu's button. A row's button pressed acts on the page, which stays, and is no longer drawn either, so focus goes back to the menu's button too, as a menu button's item returns it.

  Hidden, the panel is undrawn (`hidden`, `display: none`), so neither Tab nor a screen reader reaches its rows. Shown, it stands over the page under the button, its inline end at the button's, in the page's color inside an edge in `--field-edge` with the small radius and no shadow (Flat Ledger); the edge is over 3:1 against the page and the header's band in light and dark (1.4.11), and under forced colors it is the system's. It is at least 12rem wide and never wider than the window less 2rem (`min(12rem, 100vw - 2rem)`, 1.4.10). Its rows are blocks 2.75rem tall (2.5.8) with 1rem of inline padding. Its styling is Tailwind utilities (decision 117), logical ones only; `style.css` adds only the current button's bold, since the base rule gives every button the font around it, and the header's own placement of the panel.

  The caller's duties under WCAG 2.2 AA, since the menu does none of them: a `label` that names the group of rows (2.4.6), the same whether the panel shows or not; a `panel_id` unique on the page; rows that are links or buttons, in a list (`<ul>`) the caller passes as the panel's child. It takes no tone, since it is navigation, not a status, and no size, since it is drawn at the navigation's.

  The gallery's "Disclosure menu" demo is two menus at the end of a row, the last where the header's Settings menu stands, then a button after them: the first's row is a link to the Log, so its button isn't marked; the last's rows are a link to the gallery itself, so its button is marked, a link to the start page and a button. `features/ui/components/disclosure_menu.feature` checks Enter, Space and Escape, Tab through the rows and past the last, a row's link chosen and a row's button pressed, the marked button, the pointer resting and pressing outside, the panel's edge in light, dark and forced colors, and 320 × 256 with 200 % text; its pointer steps only move and press through `ui-tests/support/pointer.ts`, whose press the maintainer approved (2026-11-23).
