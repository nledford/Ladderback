# The progress bar

- `Progress` (a focusable `role="progressbar"` with `aria-valuenow`/`aria-valuemin`/`aria-valuemax` and an `aria-valuetext` that says the step, the percentage, the amounts and the time left, told at most every 30 s while the step stays the same, never written to a live region; a reading without a total, or of zero, leaves out `aria-valuenow` and the track, and its value text and visible text are the step, or `ui-progress-working`, never 0 %; a count past its total is 100 %, never more).
  - *The look* (#794). Tailwind utilities (decision 117), logical ones only, and no rule of its own in `style.css`: its classes are `progress/rules.rs`'s, the bar's own (`rules::bar_class`) host-tested. A Ledger Gray pill track (`bg-surface`) filling the room it is given up to 16rem, so in a table's cell it never widens its column, and a fill whose `inline-size` follows the reading, from the track's start in the page's direction, easing to each report over 0.4 s and jumping under the reduced-motion rule.
  - *Vocabulary.* It takes its `tone` and `size` from the shared vocabulary and leaves none out. The tone's class sets `--tone` on the bar, and the fill alone is drawn in it (`bg-(--tone)`), the tone's own token and never its status pair (decision 108), `Primary` by default: the track stays Ledger Gray and the step and the percentage keep the text's color, so a `Success`, `Warning` or `Error` bar never says how a run goes by color alone (1.4.1), and `Inherit` takes the text around it. The vocabulary has no default size, so the bar picks `Small` (`rules::default_size`); its size is its track's thickness, half the shared scale's length (`--size`: 0.5em, 0.75em, 1em and 1.5em), so the track grows with the reader's text. No placement: a bar fills the room its caller gives it.
  - *Forced colors.* The fill and the track opt out of the mode, which would paint both the page's background and leave the bar empty: the fill takes `CanvasText`, as the alert's border does, the text's color wherever a bar sits on Canvas (an opted-out element's `currentColor` is the author's, the spinner's finding), and the track `GrayText`, as a spinner's track does.
  - *The gallery.* The "Progress" demo, after the Text field demo and before the Table demo, so the walks that cross it stay short, shows the bar the reader advances, one of unknown size, one of a step, then one at each size and one in each tone, each 60 % done. `features/ui/components/progress.feature` checks axe and the ARIA snapshot, every bar as a stop for Tab with its focus ring around its step, track and words, its value text, the live regions silent, each size's thickness at 200 % text, each tone's fill on the Ledger Gray track with the words in the text's color in light and dark, forced colors, and every bar at 320 px with 200 % text in both directions, its fill at the track's start. The Operations page's bars (`ui-plan-progress`, `ui-operation-progress-of`) keep the defaults, `Primary` and `Small`, as before.
