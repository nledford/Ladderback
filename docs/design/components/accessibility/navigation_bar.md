# The navigation bar

- **The navigation bar** is `NavigationBar` (`components/navigation_bar.rs`, decisions [143](../../decisions/143-a-hamburger-menu-holds-the-pages-links-below-41rem.md), [152](../../decisions/152-a-one-line-header-with-a-settings-menu.md) and [160](../../decisions/160-the-navigation-bar-is-a-shared-component.md)): the header of every page. It draws the brand its caller passes, then a `<nav>` named by the caller (`label`) whose list holds a link for each page (`NavigationPage`), each a `Link` standing alone in the color of the text around it, and last a [disclosure menu](disclosure_menu.md) for the group (Settings, Log, then Sign out under a hairline in the header). The current page's link is `aria-current="page"`, bold with a 3px bar at its inline start, never told by color alone (A2); the group's button is marked `aria-current="true"` while the reader is on one of its pages.

  From 54rem up it is one line: the brand at its start, the links and the group's button at its end, on one baseline. Below 54rem a hamburger ends the brand's line: the shared `Button`, icon-only, Ghost, XLarge (3em square, 48 px at the page's text size, a finger's target), Lucide's `Menu` while its panel is hidden and `X` while it shows, named by the caller (`menu_label`, "Menu" in the header) in both states, that name also its tooltip at its inline start. `aria-expanded` says whether the panel shows and `aria-controls` names it. There the group's button isn't drawn, and its rows are rows of the bar's panel, after the pages': no menu in a menu.

  Shown, the panel stands over the page under the brand's line, the width of the bar, in the page's color inside an edge in `--field-edge` (over 3:1 against the page and the header's band in light and dark, 1.4.11; the system's under forced colors) with the small radius and no shadow (Flat Ledger). Opening and closing it moves nothing on the page. Its rows stand in as many columns of 10em as it holds (two at 390 px, one at 320 px with 200 % text), each at least 2.75rem tall with 1rem of inline padding (2.5.8), tinted Ledger Gray under the pointer; the current page's bar is a border, so forced colors keep it (in `CanvasText`), and the focus ring is drawn inside the row. Hidden, it is `display: none`, so neither Tab nor a screen reader reaches its rows.

  When it opens and closes is `components/navigation_bar/rules.rs` with the disclosure menu's rules, decided on the host. Enter, Space or a press toggles the panel. Escape anywhere in the bar closes it and returns focus to the hamburger, but only while the hamburger is drawn, and not while a tooltip shows, which that Escape hides (1.4.13).

  Focus moving to an element outside the bar closes it without moving focus (2.4.11); focus going nowhere leaves it open. A press of the pointer outside the bar closes it, the way back on a touch screen with no Escape. A link chosen in it closes it, and the route change moves focus to the new page's heading; the current page's link changes no route, so focus goes back to the hamburger (2.4.3). A route change, and the page showing no pages (the sign-in page, where the bar is the brand alone), close it too. Tab walks the hamburger, then every row in order, with no arrow keys taken and no focus trap. Nothing animates.

  Since the open panel covers the top of the page, the caller moves focus to what it shows there: a failed sign-out in the header hands focus to its alert at the top of `<main>`, so focus leaving the bar closes the panel and the alert is seen and heard (decision 160).

  The caller's duties under WCAG 2.2 AA, since the bar does none of them: a `label` naming the navigation, unique among the page's `navigation` landmarks, a `group_label` naming the group and a `menu_label` naming the hamburger, unique among the page's buttons (2.4.6); a `panel_id` unique on the page, from which the hamburger's `id` (`<panel_id>-button`) and the group's panel's (`<panel_id>-group`) are made; a brand that is a link to the start page. It takes no tone, since it is navigation, not a status, and no size, since it is drawn at the text's.

  The gallery's "Navigation bar" demo is a second bar: a brand linking to the start page, a link to the gallery itself, so marked current, and one to the Plans page, a group holding a link to the Log and a button under the hairline, and a button after the bar. `features/ui/components/navigation_bar.feature` checks the one line and the undrawn hamburger from 54rem up; below it Enter, Space and Escape, Tab through the rows and past the last, the current page's row chosen, a press outside, the panel standing over what follows it, two columns at 390 px and one at 320 × 568 px with 200 % text. `features/ui/header.feature` checks the header's own bar: its rows, its colors in light, dark and forced colors, Escape past 54rem, a sign-out from it, and at 320 × 568 px with 200 % text the page's heading inside the first screen.
