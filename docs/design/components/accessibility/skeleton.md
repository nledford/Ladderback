# The skeleton

- **The skeleton** is `Skeleton` (`components/skeleton.rs`), for the still shape of content on its way, standing where the content will. Its `shape` says what it stands for, from the reader's text: `Shape::Lines { count, last }`, lines of text, each 1em tall in the middle of a line of the text's height and the full width of the room it is given, but the last, which is `last` of it (a `Share`, a whole percent); or `Shape::Block { inline, block }`, a box in `Rem`, such as a field or a button's place, never wider than its room. So the room it takes is the content's, and the page keeps its layout when the content comes. It has no circle or avatar shape, since the app shows no pictures of people, and no image or video placeholder.

  What it draws is `skeleton/rules.rs`, decided on the host: its class, and the inline size of each bar, or the block's two lengths. Its types can't hold no lines, a last line longer than its room or a negative length: `count` is a `NonZeroU8`, so it draws one line at least; a `Share` above 100 % is the whole line, the one clamp, made once where the share is built; a `Rem` is a count of tenths (the shared length of `variants.rs`, which a table's columns take too). It is a `<span>` of bars laid out as a column, so it stands in a room of its own, as wide as the content it stands for, never inside a line of text or a row that sizes it by its content; its bars stand at the start of their room, in the page's direction.

  It is flat, Pencil Gray (`--skeleton`) with the small corner (`--radius-small`): 1.61:1 on the page and 1.47:1 on Ledger Gray, 1.69:1 and 1.46:1 in dark colors, so one gray shows on the page and on the header, a toast or the pause banner alike. A skeleton carries no information, so no WCAG minimum applies (1.4.11 is for what a reader needs to see to understand), but a skeleton no one can see says nothing of its wait: `just test-contrast` holds both pairs to 1.4:1, a floor for visibility. The existing grays fall short of it: Ledger Gray is 1.10:1 on the page, the empty badge's gray 1.24:1. Under forced colors the system drops the fill, so a 1px edge in `GrayText` keeps its shape in view.

  - *Still.* It never moves, in any motion setting: no shimmer, no pulse, no transition. A skeleton starts by itself beside content the reader can use and may stay past 5 s on a slow daemon, and nothing on the page could pause it, the spinner's reason (2.2.2, Pause, Stop, Hide; the spinner's notes above).

  Its look is Tailwind utilities (decision 117), logical ones only (#931): `rules::Shape::class` returns its classes and its bars' column (`flex flex-col items-start`), and `rules::BAR` each bar's, Pencil Gray through `tailwind.css`'s `bg-skeleton`. `style.css` keeps one rule, a line's margins, half the difference between `1lh` and `1em`, which no named utility sets and an arbitrary value would hide in a class name.

  The caller's duties, which the skeleton can't meet for it:

  - **Words.** It is never a stand-in for words: the page says the wait in words ("Loading…"), visible, as it does without one, on a line just before the region and outside it, since a screen reader may leave a busy region unread. Where the content's size is known before it comes, the region keeps its height, and the content may take the words' line when it comes (the gallery's plan name does). A page's first load can't know how many rows are coming, so there the content takes the place of the words and the region both, and what follows it may move.
  - **Busy.** The region it stands for carries `aria-busy="true"` until the content arrives.
  - **Arrival.** The content takes the skeleton's place without moving focus, and nothing announces it: the announcer stays the page's (4.1.3, A6).

  *A page's first load* meets these duties through `first_load` (`components.rs`), one helper for every page: while the daemon's first answer is on its way, it draws the words ("Loading…") on a line, then, just after them, the region the answer fills, a `<div aria-busy="true">` holding the still shape of what is coming, an `Awaited` (`skeleton/rules.rs`, decided on the host), both in one `<div>`, so the page's layout sees one element. A table is its header and three rows, a line per column; a form is each field's label above a 20 rem field, then its button; words, a list or a few buttons, whose shape is known only once they come, are two lines of text.

  The shape is the content's kind, not its exact layout: a table of row headers, the controls above a table, or a radio group standing as a form's field take the nearest of the three. The page draws it where its content will stand and drops it whole when the answer comes, or when the reading reaches no daemon. Once it is dropped, nothing stays busy or says the wait; focus stays where it was, and nothing is announced. "Still waiting…" stays words. `features/ui/first_load.feature` checks a table's and a form's, busy and then not, the words just before the busy region and outside it, axe-core clean, and that the Snapshots page stops waiting once its reading of the repositories reaches no daemon.

  Taken from daisyUI's and Flowbite's skeletons, the references: a flat gray shape where the content will stand, and lines of text with a shorter last one. Not taken: their shimmer and pulse (*Still*, above), and their circle, avatar, image and video shapes.

  The gallery's "Skeleton" demo (`section#skeleton-demo`, an id that finds it under a pseudolocale too), after the Button demo and before the Text field demo, shows three lines standing for a paragraph, a block standing for a field, two lines on Ledger Gray, and a region that is busy with its skeleton after the line "Loading the demo plan…", with a toggle that brings the plan in the skeleton's place and its name in the words' line. The table's own loading state keeps its spinner, its words beside it, just before the busy region where the table will stand and outside it, as the Table's `Sentences::loading` says.

  `features/ui/components/skeleton.feature` checks axe and the ARIA snapshot, which has no skeleton in it, no stop for Tab, the busy region and its words just before it and outside it, the arrival keeping the region's height and focus, the stillness in either motion setting, the lines' lengths and heights right to left too, the block at 320 px with 200 % text, the fill and its 1.4:1 against the page and Ledger Gray in light and dark colors, and the edge under forced colors.
