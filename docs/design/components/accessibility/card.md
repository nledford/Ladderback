# The card

- **The card** is `Card` (`components/card.rs`): the shared frame of a summary of one thing that stands on its own, under The Summary Card Rule in DESIGN.md. Every card is one; the machine card ([decision 175](../../decisions/175-a-card-per-machine-on-the-hubs-dashboard.md)) is the first to compose it, and a new use is a decision.

  It has three parts, in this order. Its **title**, the header's only part for now: a heading at the caller's `level`, 2 or 3, which is required, whose words name the `<article>` (`aria-labelledby`). The heading takes focus from a script only (`tabindex="-1"`), as when a part of the card that held focus goes, so it is never a stop for Tab. Its **body**, the caller's parts, stacked at one gap inside one padding. And its **actions**, where the caller gives any: a row of controls at the card's foot and its inline end, which wraps onto more lines where the card is narrow, at 320 px with 200 % text (1.4.10). A badge beside the title waits for the first card that needs one, with its own decision.

  The card itself is never a control: never a link, a button or a stop for Tab, nor inside one, so no part of it but the controls the caller puts in it takes a press, and a keyboard user passes through those alone, in the order they stand; a card without actions or controls in its body has nothing that takes focus. A whole card as one link was rejected (decision 175): its name would be every word in it, and a control inside it couldn't be reached as itself.

  It is flat: the page's color through it, inside a 1px edge in Field Edge with the small corner (`--radius-small`), 1rem of padding and no shadow (Flat Ledger). It is never filled, striped or edged in a status color, and takes no tone and no size: a status is a badge's words inside it, drawn at the page's text size. The caller sets the cards in a list with `CARDS`: a grid of columns at least 18rem wide, or the page's whole width where it is narrower, which wraps by its own width (1.4.10). Each card fills its row's height. Its styling is Tailwind utilities (decision 117), logical ones only.

  The caller's duties under WCAG 2.2 AA, since the card does none of them: a `heading_id` unique on the page; the heading's level in the page's outline (1.3.1); controls named by the thing they act on, as "Open demo-laptop" is (2.4.6); and never a card inside a control, nor a handler on it that takes a press.

  The gallery's "Card" demo is two cards in that list at level 3: one of words alone, and one whose actions, a button and a link, follow its words. `features/ui/components/card.feature` checks the article named by its title at its level, the heading's focus from a script, that the card is no control and that one without actions holds nothing that takes focus, Tab through the actions only and in order, the actions at the foot and the inline end at 320 px with 200 % text and at 1280 px, and the flat frame, with no stripe or shadow, in light and dark colors with axe-core; the gallery's axe-core and ARIA snapshot rows pin the rest. Level 2 is the dashboard's, which `features/ui/hub_dashboard.feature` checks.
