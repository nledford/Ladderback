- **The review dialog** is `ReviewDialog` (`components/review_dialog.rs`), a seventh component drawn with `ModalDialog` (#989), so it keeps that dialog's wrap of Tab from its last stop to its first and of Shift-Tab from its first to its last, its late `close` event left alone and its opens counted by the announcer. It is titled at level 3, starts on its dismiss button (or `starts_on`), and returns focus to `returns_to`, the control that asked for the review. Its dismiss button and Escape (`on_cancel`) close it through `open`. It takes `working` (while the page starts the run: Confirm and the dismiss button carry `aria-disabled` and ignore clicks, Escape does nothing, and a paragraph with `aria-live="polite"` says what is going on) and `fallback` (where focus goes when the control that opened the dialog is gone). The paragraph is not `role="status"`, because the page keeps exactly one `status` region, the announcer's.

  Chromium may close the dialog on a second Escape in spite of the prevented `cancel`; the page keeps its own visible "Starting the forget…" line, so that is not silent.

  A dialog closed and opened again before its `close` event comes, as an Escape and then Enter on the button that opened it can be in Chromium, counts that closing gone as it opens, and the late event, finding the dialog open, does nothing, so it never closes the dialog under the reader.

  A review with two ways to go ahead passes the second as `also`, a second `Confirmation` rendered after the confirm button, so the dismiss button stays first and the second choice last; under `working` it carries `aria-disabled` and ignores clicks, as the others do. Settings' restart dialog (§4.4) passes **Restart now** so while operations are running; the gallery's "Demo review to confirm" has **Confirm the demo review now**, and `features/ui/components/review_dialog.feature` pins the order Cancel, confirm, second choice, back to Cancel.

  A review that can't be confirmed yet passes `held`, a signal of the reason or none. While it holds a reason, the confirm button and `also` carry `aria-disabled` and `aria-describedby` naming a paragraph, `{returns_to}-held`, that shows the reason after the dialog's body; a press says the reason in the `working` paragraph (cleared, then set on the next tick), and does nothing else. The gallery's "Demo review on hold" is held for good; `features/ui/components/review_dialog.feature` checks its description, the press and that Escape still closes it.
